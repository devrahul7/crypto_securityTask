Cryptographic Pipeline in Action

01

Transport Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS)

Data entered is serialized into JSON and packaged inside a TLS record encrypted with TLS_AES_256_GCM_SHA384. Eavesdroppers on the wire capture only pseudorandom bytes.

02

At-Rest Email Encryption (AES-256-GCM)

The backend extracts the email and generates a unique 12-byte IV. It runs AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, producing ciphertext and an authentication tag. Plaintext email is erased from persistence.

03

Salted Password Hashing (Bcrypt Cost 12)

The password is salted with 128 bits of entropy and passed through 4,096 cryptographic hashing rounds (212). It is impossible to reverse, even with rainbow tables or dictionary attacks.

04

Zero-Leakage Blind Indexing (HMAC-SHA256)

A deterministic keyed HMAC blind token allows O(1) user lookups and duplicate detection without decrypting all records or leaking email hashes.
